# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Personal branding — Rifat Dhiya Ul Lail himself, professional network, recruiters, fellow developers. The site is Rifat's professional identity online.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Rifat's skills, projects, and professional journey as a web developer. Credibility, clarity, and a lasting first impression.

## Brand Personality

Clean, modern, professional. Confident without being loud. Warm without being sentimental.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI templates (the "I used AI to make this" look)
- Overly busy or cluttered layouts — no sensory overload
- Corporate/formal company-profile style — this is personal, not institutional

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — every element earns its place. No filler.
2. **Personal without being casual** — professional warmth, not social-media informality.
3. **Performance is a feature** — fast loads, smooth interactions, no unnecessary JavaScript.
4. **Accessibility is non-negotiable** — high-priority WCAG AA+ compliance: contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, screen reader support.
5. **Timeless over trendy** — avoid design patterns that scream "2024" or "2025".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA+ priority. High contrast, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, semantic HTML, aria labels, skip link, focus-visible on all interactive elements.
